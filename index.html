The three numbers

Chance you win (equity)
How often your hand wins if it's played to the end. The simulation estimates it.
Pot odds
Your share of the final pot. Calling $50 into a $150 pot means paying 50 of 200, which is 25%.
Expected value
If your chance of winning is higher than the pot odds, calling makes money over time. If it's lower, it loses money.
