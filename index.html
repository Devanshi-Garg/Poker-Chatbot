Texas Hold'em Beginner Cheat Sheet

Hand Rankings (Highest to Lowest)

1. Royal Flush: A, K, Q, J, 10 same suit
2. Straight Flush: 5 consecutive same suit
3. Four of a Kind: 4 cards same rank
4. Full House: 3 of a kind + 1 pair
5. Flush: Any 5 cards same suit
6. Straight: 5 consecutive rank cards
7. Three of a Kind: 3 cards same rank
8. Two Pair: 2 distinct pairs
9. One Pair: 2 cards same rank
10. High Card: Highest single card

Key Concepts Glossary

Pot Odds: The ratio of the current pot size to the cost of a contemplated call. If calling costs $50 into a $150 pot, pot odds are 50 / (150+50) = 25%.

Win Equity: Your mathematical percentage probability of winning the hand by the river based on simulation.

Expected Value (EV): The average amount won or lost per bet over the long run. If Win Equity > Pot Odds, EV is positive (+EV).
