Texas Hold'em Beginner Guide & Hand Rankings

Poker Hand Rankings (Highest to Lowest)

  1. Royal Flush: A, K, Q, J, 10 all of same suit
  2. Straight Flush: Five consecutive cards of same suit
  3. Four of a Kind: Four cards of equal rank
  4. Full House: Three of a kind + a Pair
  5. Flush: Any five cards of same suit
  6. Straight: Five consecutive cards of any suit
  7. Three of a Kind: Three cards of equal rank
  8. Two Pair: Two distinct pairs
  9. One Pair: Two cards of equal rank
  10. High Card: Highest rank card in hand
What is Win Equity?

Your calculated percentage chance of having the best 5-card poker hand at showdown out of thousands of random board completions.

What are Pot Odds?

The ratio of the call amount relative to the total pot size. If your Win Equity % exceeds Pot Odds %, making the call is mathematically (+EV) profitable long-term.
